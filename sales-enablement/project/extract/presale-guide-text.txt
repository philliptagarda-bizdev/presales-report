customer toward purchase

The Value-First Sales Mindset

-- 30 of 35 --

The play
Our strategy relies on the principle that "customer education beats persuasion"
We do not just sell data bundles; We understand the customer’s entire sentiment
immediately map the customer's travel frustrations to our unique, industry-leading
features, the goal is to effectively communicate how we can help solve their pain.
Pain: Wasting money on fixed bundles ➔ Solution: Ultimate Flexibility. Emphasize
our Pay-per-Day Customization slider bar, allowing them to pay for the exact days
they need
Pain: Hassle of new QR codes every trip ➔ Solution: Permanent Connectivity.
Highlight One eSIM for Life, a single profile installation that works across 150+
destinations forever
Pain: Losing access to home numbers ➔ Solution: Zero Interruption. Reassure
them that our eSIM Runs Alongside their Physical SIM, keeping WhatsApp and 2FA
active
Pain: Uncertainty of when to activate ➔ Solution: Risk-Free Planning. Explain our
365-Day Activation Window, letting them buy now with zero pressure to activate
until they travel

Effective Communication
The P.A.Y.S. Consultative Framework - Quick, Feature-First Value Selling
We ideally use the P.A.Y.S. framework to control the conversation and drive
urgency without sounding like a script:
P - Profile (Identify Pain Points): Get to the root of their need in 2-3 messages
Ask direct context questions like, "What was the most frustrating part of getting
online on your last trip?"
A - Assess & Articulate Value (Lead with Solution): Instantly present the unique
holiday.com feature that solves the exact pain point they just mentioned
Y - Yield Incentives (Create Urgency): Drop the high-impact offer immediately after
establishing value
Remind them that the “50% off your first purchase” hook is strictly limited to our first
500,000 travelers
S - Secure Commitment (Close the Sale): Confirm the benefit works for them ("So,
getting the exact days you need works for you?"), then use the limited discount to
push an incentive-driven close

Closing the sale
Master the Pivot: Strategic CTA Placement - When and How to Drop Your
Call-to-Action
A Call-to-Action (CTA): Effective CTAs should never feel like an interruption. To
maximize conversion rates, we need to deploy strong CTAs at two primary
conversational pivots
Pivot 1: Post-Value Articulation (After the 'A' in P.A.Y.S.)
The Goal: Capitalize on the "aha!" moment right after you match their pain point to
our feature
Example: "That hassle is gone with holiday.com. Click here to install your
permanent profile now, so you never worry about downloading another
QR code again."
Pivot 2: Post-Incentive Yield (After the 'Y' in P.A.Y.S.)
The Goal: Leverage Fear of Missing Out (FOMO) right after mentioning the 50% off
promo
Example: "The 50% off deal ends at 500,000 travelers and we're nearing the
limit. Configure your days now to lock in the discount before it's gone."
