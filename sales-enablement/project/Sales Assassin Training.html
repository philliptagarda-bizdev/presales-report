WHAT IS A PRESALE INTERACTION?
Presale interactions occur when:
  • Customers inquire about eSIM services
  • The customer does not yet have an active data plan
  • The conversation presents an opportunity to guide the customer toward purchase
Your role is to understand the customer’s needs and recommend the most suitable plan.

Examples of CTA
  • Recommending a plan
  • Sharing a purchase link
  • Offering to guide the customer through the purchase process
A strong CTA helps customers

  • Take the next step
  • Complete their purchase

GREETING & PROMOTION
Greeting & Acknowledgement
  • Be friendly and approachable
  • Confirm you can assist with eSIM-related questions
  • Avoid overwhelming the customer with too much info immediately
Promotion & Setting Expectations
Position the promotions to prevent disconnections:
“Yes, we do offer a data plan that works in China. We’re also currently running a 50% discount for the first 500,000 travelers…

If you’d like, I can help check whether our service will work well for your trip and guide you to the best option.”
holiday.com
DISCOVERY / PROBING QUESTIONS
Ask 1–2 questions at a time, personalized to their responses. Avoid sounding like a form.
  • Destination: “Which country or countries will you be traveling to?”
  • Travel Dates: “When will you be traveling, and how long will your trip be?”
  • Device Compatibility: “Are you using an eSIM-compatible and carrier-unlocked device?”
  • Use Case: “How do you intend to use the data — browsing, streaming?”
holiday.com
RECOMMEND THE BEST PLAN
Best Practices
  • Highlight why the plan fits their needs
  • Avoid listing too many plans
  • Focus on 1–2 recommendations
Example
“Based on your trip, our Japan 7-Day Unlimited Data plan would be a great option. It should ensure that you have enough connectivity for browsing, messaging, and streaming during your stay.”

PRESALE TICKET INTERACTIONS
Ticket interactions occur when:
  • Customers inquire about eSIM services but disconnect before purchase
  • Customers inquire via email ticket
  • The conversation presents an ongoing opportunity through our 2-touch follow-up sequence
Your role: build on your initial understanding, adhere to data privacy rules, and proactively recommend the most suitable plan through timely, value-driven ticket responses.
holiday.com
PRESALE FOLLOW-UP SEQUENCE
Use the macros below in your follow-up cadence:
First (30 min – 1 hr): [HD] Presale Follow-Up 1: Initial Contact
Summarize what they need, recommend a specific plan, mention the 6-month refund rule.
Day 3: [HD] Presale Follow-Up 2: Third Day Check-In
Offer more help with their decision; include a 60-second setup guide.
Day 7 (with account): [HD] Presale Follow-Up 3: With Account
Final reminder; invite last questions, offer future assistance.
Day 7 (no account): [HD] Presale Follow-Up 3: No Account Yet
Final reminder; provide a link to our Privacy Policy.
