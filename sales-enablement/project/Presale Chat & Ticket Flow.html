Defining the moment

What Is a Presale
Interaction?

A presale is interest + no active plan + an opening. The instant you see all three, your role changes — you’re no longer answering a question, you’re understanding a need and recommending the most suitable plan.

Most chats in your queue are already presales. Train your eye: the opportunity is almost always there. The only question is whether you see it in time.

1Interest

The customer asks about our eSIM service.

2No active plan

They don’t yet have a live data plan.

3An opening

The conversation lets you guide them toward a purchase.

Never end on a full stop

The Call to Action

Every presale chat should end with a clear CTA — the next step, made obvious and easy. Without one, even a perfect chat leaks. With one, you close the loop.

The rule: end on a door, not a full stop. “Shall I share the link?” · “Would you like me to set this up with you?”
Three forms of a CTA

① Recommend a specific plan   ② Share a purchase link   ③ Offer to walk them through checkout.

Stages one to three

Greet · Hook · Ask

1Greeting & Acknowledgement

Be friendly and approachable. Confirm you can help with their eSIM questions — and don’t overwhelm them all at once. A warm hello buys you the whole conversation.

2Promotion & Setting Expectations

Right after their first question, position the offer to keep attention and prevent disconnects.

“Yes, we offer a plan that works in China. We’re also running 50% off for our first 500,000 travellers. If you’d like, share your travel details and I’ll guide you to the best option.”

3Discovery / Probing Questions

Understand needs before recommending. Ask about destination, travel dates, device compatibility, and use case — one or two at a time, personalised. Never a long list, never a form.

holiday.com
Stages four to eight

Recommend · Guide · Close

4Recommend

Tie the plan to their words: “Based on your trip, our Japan 7-day unlimited plan would be a great fit.” One or two recommendations — never a menu.

5Reassure

If they hesitate: instant delivery, no physical SIM, easy install, and 24/7 live chat support.

6Guide to Purchase

Download the holiday.com app → sign up → select destination → choose duration → enter payment → checkout.

Critical: travellers have 365 days to install & activate. To avoid early activation, only tap “Turn on eSIM” after arrival. Say this clearly — it prevents a very common mistake.
7Offer Support

“If anything comes up during install or travel, our team is here anytime.”

8Close Warmly

“In the meantime, is there anything else I can help with?”

The P.A.Y.S. Framework

P

Profile

Find the pain point in 2–3 messages.

“What was the most frustrating part of getting online on your last trip?”

A

Assess & Articulate

Instantly present the one feature that solves the pain they named. Lead with the solution.

Y

Yield Incentives

Create urgency: 50% off is limited to our first 500,000 travellers.

S

Secure Commitment

Confirm the benefit fits, then use the discount to guide a warm close.

“So, getting the exact days you need works for you?”

Strategic CTA placement

Master the Pivot

A great CTA never feels like an interruption. Deploy your strongest ones at two conversational pivots.

Pivot 1 · After the “A”
Post-value — capitalise on the “aha” the moment you match pain to feature.
“That hassle is gone with holiday.com. Install your permanent profile now, so you never download another QR code again.”
Pivot 2 · After the “Y”
Post-incentive — leverage genuine F.O.M.O. right after the 50% offer.
“The 50% deal ends at 500,000 travellers, and we’re nearing the limit. Configure your days now to lock in the discount.”
Ethics rule: F.O.M.O. must always be true — real limits, real lead times. Honest urgency converts; fake urgency destroys trust.
