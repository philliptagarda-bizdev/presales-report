The anatomy of the first pitch
ANATOMY OF THE
ORIGINAL PITCH

The Serpent utilized core principles that drive modern sales funnels:

Identifying the "Pain Point": Gap between where Eve was and where she could be.

Reframing the Objection: Serpent used Objection Handling to reframe "risk" as opportunity.

The Value Proposition: Focus on ROI of transformation over cost of transaction.

Creating Urgency: Triggered the first recorded instance of FOMO.

Sales core principles
FROM EDEN TO
BOARDROOMS

To understand sales is to understand the Apple and the Serpent as metaphors for the buyer-seller dynamic:

1. The Apple (The Solution): Every product is an "Apple." It represents the promise of a "New Better"—a bridge to a desired state of being.

2. The Serpent (The Consultant): Represents the Catalyst. Moving a prospect from "Status Quo" to "Action" through narrative.

3. The Result (The Exchange): Eve traded security for an upgrade. This highlights that value is subjective—therefore, perceived.
